## Required rules

20 invites, 25 bottles/user, 10 processing attempts/user/day, 500 KB originals and cutouts, 250 KB condition photos, upload stop at 800 MB. Shelves private by default with opt-in sharing within invited members. One-for-one Philippines local/shipping trades, no payment. Reserve only on mutual acceptance; transfer only on both receipts. Cancellation requires both before dispatch/receipt; afterwards dispute. Expire unaccepted proposals after seven days. No automatic receipt or admin ownership override. Original/trade image versions immutable. Keep private storage, anonymous denial and server-only credentials. Resume drafts and cache collections offline. Purge abandoned uploads after seven days. No paid upgrades, keepalive hacks, or hidden local-server fallback in beta.
